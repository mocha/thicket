# The thicket name and logo

The software is free (AGPL-3.0, see LICENSE). The name **thicket**, the
domain **readthicket.com**, and the thicket logo are not: they identify the
project and the instance its authors run, and they are reserved.

You may:

- run the software, modified or not, for yourself, your friends, or as a
  service, and say truthfully that it is "powered by thicket" or "a fork of
  thicket";
- use the name to refer to the project, link to it, and write about it.

You may not:

- call your hosted service "thicket", or use a name, domain, or logo that
  makes it look like readthicket.com or the project itself;
- use the logo as your own.

This is the same arrangement Mastodon, WordPress, and Mozilla use: anyone can
run the code, nobody can pretend to be the project. If you are unsure, ask.
